## After 15 Years of Making Music, Cleveland's Akashic Monk Is Finally Making Moves

Some artists blow up overnight. **Akashic Monk** took the long road, and that's exactly why this moment hits different.

For fifteen years, the Cleveland, Ohio hip-hop artist has been writing, recording and building in the 216. The official catalog on Apple Music tells the story of the last ten: **41 releases and 113 tracks since 2016**, all independent and all released under the artist's own label, **Akashic Entertainment®**. Now, with a 22-track album out and a new one on the way, the Monk is finally making motion.

### A Cleveland sound, built from the ground up

Akashic Monk's music blends **heavy 808 low-end, spiritual lyricism and Midwest cadences** with pop and rock hooks. The result speaks to rap fans and crosses over to listeners who don't usually play hip-hop. It's music for late-night drives down the Shoreway and early mornings grinding toward something bigger.

The catalog shows the growth: the early *Road to 2020 Hits & Singles* collections, the darker *DarkLight*, the 2020 album *Soul Savior* (featuring NoBa'D, DNA88, EDC Jay, I.Am.PJ and Th3 Eye), the mystic *Thoughts of a Wizard* (2022), and a steady run of singles like "What I'm Grinding For," "God Is King," "Monk Mode," "Akashic Agent" and "Empire."

### 2026: the year of motion

2026 is the most productive year of Akashic Monk's career so far: **eleven new singles and a 22-track album, *Static Signal***, released in July. Standouts include:

- **"UnderDog Dreams"** (July 2026): https://music.apple.com/us/album/underdog-dreams-single/6787865672

- ***Static Signal*** (full album, 22 tracks): https://music.apple.com/us/album/static-signal/6795564641

### The crypto-coding gamer rapper

What sets Akashic Monk apart from other upcoming Cleveland artists is everything happening off the mic. The Monk codes, produces AI-driven music videos, runs the *AVR Crypto Castle Build Series* on YouTube, documented the grind in a 36-episode vlog series, and completed a **100-day streaming marathon**. On top of that, Akashic Monk is building ***Order of the Akasha***, a fantasy universe of guardians, mystic warriors and a living library of all knowledge, told through music, film and gaming.

The motto says it all: **Create. Build. Evolve.**

### What's next: *American Blueprint*

Everything points to ***American Blueprint***, the new album arriving **Winter 2026**. It's a plan drawn up from the ground floor in Cleveland, by an artist who spent fifteen years proving you don't need permission to build.

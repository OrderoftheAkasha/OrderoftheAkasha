## Be interview-ready (before the first yes)
- **Setup:** decent USB mic, headphones, quiet room, stable internet, good light facing you. Test on Riverside/Zoom/StreamYard.
- **Three stories to tell:** how you started in 2016 · the hardest moment staying independent · what *American Blueprint* means.
- **Freestyle bank:** have 16 bars ready on gaming, coding, crypto and Cleveland, then weave in the host's topic live.
- **After every episode:** clip 3 vertical shorts (TikTok/Reels/Shorts), tag the show, add it to the website's Interviews tab, and thank the host.

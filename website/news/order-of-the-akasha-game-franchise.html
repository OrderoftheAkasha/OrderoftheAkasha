Order of the Akasha: An Independent Artist's Plan to Turn a Rap Universe Into a Game Franchise

Some indie game projects start with a prototype. Order of the Akasha started with a mythology, two novels and a decade of music.

Cleveland hip-hop artist and author Akashic Monk is developing Order of the Akasha as a multi-platform franchise: books, music, film and games built on the same world. The foundation is already public. There are two Kindle novels (The Book of Scrolls and Origin's of Akir), a catalog of 41 music releases, and a first batch of trailer concept art: the Mystic Warrior, the Guardian and the Akashic Library.

The world design is made for games. The Akasha is a fractured cosmic force spread across realms, which means natural levels and biomes: cybernetic cities, elemental realms, ancient libraries. There are two playable-feeling heroes with distinct powers: Akir's DarkLight Halo and Ayi's elemental energies. And the books already contain game-ready set pieces: relic hunts, epic battles and "races of fate."

Akashic Monk brings more than lore to the table. The Monk codes, makes AI-assisted music videos and has a built-in soundtrack catalog, so the music and the world can grow together. The motto for the project: Create. Build. Evolve.

Game studios, artists and developers interested in the world can reach out directly.

10 Things to Know About Akashic Monk, Cleveland's Rapper, Author and World-Builder

  1. Cleveland-born and built. Akashic Monk is an independent hip-hop artist from Cleveland, Ohio: the 216.
  2. Fifteen years of making music. There are 41 official releases and 113 tracks on Apple Music since 2016.
  3. Owns the label. Every release comes out on the artist's own Gx3 ChaosRecords® / Akashic Entertainment®.
  4. Published author. Order of the Akasha: The Book of Scrolls is on Kindle and in paperback, and Origin's of Akir is on Kindle.
  5. The books and music connect. The 2020 album DarkLight and the novel's DarkLight Halo are part of the same universe.
  6. A game franchise in the works. Order of the Akasha is being built as a multi-platform world, with trailer concept art already created.
  7. Crypto-coding gamer rapper. The Monk codes, makes AI music videos and runs a crypto build series on YouTube.
  8. Built in public. That includes a 36+ episode vlog and a 100-day streaming marathon.
  9. Big 2026. Eleven singles and a 22-track album, Static Signal.
  10. What's next. The album American Blueprint arrives Winter 2026.

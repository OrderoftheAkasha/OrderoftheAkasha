Bio

SHORT BIO (50 WORDS)

Akashic Monk is an independent Cleveland hip-hop artist and founder of Akashic Entertainment®. With 40+ releases since 2016, Akashic Monk blends heavy 808s, spiritual lyricism and Midwest cadences with pop and rock energy. The 22-track album Static Signal is out now; the new album American Blueprint arrives Winter 2026.

Born and built in the 216, Akashic Monk makes music the Cleveland way: independent, relentless and bigger than the block. Since the first releases in 2016, the Monk has put out more than forty official records, including the projects DarkLight, Soul Savior, Thoughts of a Wizard and 2026's 22-track Static Signal.

The sound pairs heavy low-end and dark synth textures with spiritual, self-made lyricism: tracks about manifestation, hustle and rising above. The same songs pull in pop hooks and rock energy, so the audience reaches well past traditional rap fans. Alongside the music, Akashic Monk runs Akashic Entertainment®, documents the grind in a 36-episode vlog series, completed a 100-day streaming marathon, and is building Order of the Akasha, a fantasy universe told through music, film and gaming.

The next album, American Blueprint, is the plan drawn up from the ground floor, a Cleveland artist's blueprint for making it on your own terms. It arrives Winter 2026.

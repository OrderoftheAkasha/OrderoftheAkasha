Handling & storage

VarietyFlavor & textureHow to cookStorage
Lion's ManeMild, sweet, seafood-like; tender and meatyTear or slice thick; press and sear in butter or oil until golden. Great breaded.Paper bag, fridge 34–38°F. Best within 5–7 days.
ChestnutNutty, earthy; firm, crunchy stemsCook whole or halved; holds shape in risotto, braises and stir-fry.Paper bag, fridge. Best within 5–7 days.
OysterSavory, delicate; velvetySeparate clusters; high heat to crisp edges. Excellent fried.Paper bag, fridge. Best within 4–5 days.

Tip: don't wash under running water. Brush clean or give a quick rinse and pat dry just before cooking.
